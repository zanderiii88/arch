# 0.100.0 - A few familiar faces

Cumulative over complete installed 0.83 or later. Replace matching files, merge assets and retain everything else. Nothing pushed or deployed. No raster replacements, ferry timetable changes, fishing/golf changes or inventory quantity migrations. Main/Story-test save keys remain.

## Kyra and conversations

Kyra uses selected real Nat-ferry arrivals on Old Rayne after the first night. Eligible days use the repeating four-day pattern's first two days, and still require the actual scheduled or phone-recalled ferry. She is not permanently stationed on the dock. She rides aboard, follows the native gangway/dock route, pauses for conversation and walks back with a twenty-game-minute boarding margin. Her visible presence, talk target and collision follow this transient actor. Visitors do not alter travel access or schedules. Night, another island, owner/slot/day replacement and away vessels clear the visit. This is session-only, not a new persistent quest or reward.

Warm-paper speech buttons appear for new Nat/Katie/Bruce introductions, Kyra's introduction/notes or ready research, and relevant Jamie/Ben introductions, lessons and project handovers. Ordinary repeated chat does not get a bubble. Unknown names stay neutral in accessibility labels. Buttons follow the native actor, avoid the map activity controls and each other, hide through overlays/travel and call native walk-to-talk handlers. Captured controls reject owner/slot/scene replacement or no-longer-relevant people. This is a scoped conversation cue pass, not an exhaustive new dialogue system.

The Pinewatch hut's final player-based repaint no longer covers the already-sorted NPC layer. Doorway actors in the threshold strip remain in front; actors genuinely north of the hut remain occluded. Shared complete native sprites and existing paths are retained.

## Field notes and gatherings

New stories start without a field or medicine notebook. Kyra introduces herself and offers spare field notes; finishing that conversation adds them once to Key items and enables the journal's field notes and herbs. Interrupted handovers can be offered again. Earlier wildlife discoveries continue recording. Existing legacy saves with prior research introductions, medicine pages or herb deliveries retain earned notebook access. Explicit new-story flags remain respected. No discoveries, pages, research payments or herb quantities are cleared.

Early gathered herbs are visible as icon/quantity buttons under Gatherings in Backpack. Tapping shows the botanical name and quantity. They remain in their established protected stock record and 24-sprig gathering capacity, separate from the twelve supply slots; no new transfer, sale or bin behavior is added. Native patch distances and three-day regrowth remain. Nepeta cataria keeps its later catnip reveal.

Journal collection and older Island notes shortcuts are gated consistently. Before the handover, herb access routes to Backpack. Later herb notes show discovered plants only. Cottage notes require the introduction and at least one earned page, and show earned pages only. No empty medicine notebook or unknown Mini/Mona pages are exposed through that feature. Existing actual cottage encounters remain.
