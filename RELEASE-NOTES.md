# Archipelago 0.48 — Coastal collections

The approved twelve-creature art batch is now used in catches, inventory icons and journal portraits: mackerel, herring, pollock, sea bass, plaice, flounder, wrasse, sand goby, shore crab, hermit crab, prawn and octopus. Herring, plaice and sand goby are new species; earlier species and discoveries remain. Other existing wildlife retains its previous art.

Old Rayne, Pinewatch, Reedhaven and Emberstone have different weighted fishing rosters. The collection journal shows undiscovered silhouettes for the new art batch, discoveries, largest measured specimens and recorded islands. Older records retain their discoveries and best fish sizes; missing historical locations are not invented. Returning animals keeps their records. Research and the earlier journal/rewards remain accessible through the new journal.

Fish signal a struggle with a twitch and water rings before pulling harder. Constant reeling can break the line; releasing lets tension recover. The first two welcome-gift fish cannot break free, but still require releasing when tension is high. Missed bites remain possible.

Katie sells a Coastal rod for 55 coins. The Tidekeeper rod costs 145 coins and appears after the community timber project. Purchases equip the rod; switch among owned rods from Journal > Rods. Upgrades reduce tension, improve recovery and speed reeling. Existing strong line still helps.

Small procedural tail and leg/arm-tip motions keep the body anchored; ambient-animation settings disable them. These are subtle motions from the approved sprite art, not full bespoke multi-frame swimming cycles.

Validation: collection save/load, catch and release records, gift reservation, purchase/equip guards, island rosters, actual fishing-update loss/catch integration, pots and pool measurements, 100 attentive fishing simulations per rod, prior progression/ferry/sleep regression checks, and sprite/catch-size renders. Careful baseline sea-bass simulations averaged about 22 seconds with the starter rod, 17 with Coastal and 15 with Tidekeeper. Phone touch feel, balance, sound and vibration still need playtesting.

This cumulative package includes 0.35 onward. Temporary exploration mode remains off. The Mini/Mona story expansion is still future work.
