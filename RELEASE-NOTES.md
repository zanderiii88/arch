# Archipelago 0.17.0 — People at work

Early development build, 22 October 2026. Not a final build, release candidate or final playtest.

- Ben sketches, walks to inspect the spring channel and checks his plans.
- Jamie chops fallen branches at a stump, carries logs to the stack and visits the repair area.
- Iris watches the water and writes field notes. Nat checks ropes and post, Katie arranges stock, and Bruce inspects his wares while their services are present.
- Short walking loops use scene paths and collision checks. Characters yield to Hannah and wait when selected, then resume their interrupted activity after conversation. Their matching artwork gains a walking stride and small work animations.
- Conversations stay along the bottom with names at the top right. Returning acquaintances can comment on their current activity.
- Menus, conversations, repairs and shrine moments pause routines. Late-night work becomes quiet rest. Decorative animation settings are respected.
- Background NPC work does not create free supplies, alter collection records, or advance story progress. Existing saves load with safe starting positions; inventory, research payments and the first shrine glow remain intact.
- Build plan updated with future Minnie/Mona home-and-exploration routines and Euan's loan/purchase camera, photography, album and framed photographs.

Only Old Rayne Isle and Pinewatch are currently playable. Minnie, Mona, Euan and the camera are planned for future island releases. This build uses the existing woodland and landing close-ups; it does not add NPCs to the clean island overworld.

Temporary ferry exploration remains enabled after the first sleep. Shop, trader and postal schedules retain their rules. Restore intended passenger schedules, island progression gates and emergency-phone rules before final release; see FUTURE-BUILDS.md.

Install: extract this complete ZIP into the repository root, replacing matching files, then commit and push. No intermediate releases are needed. No automatic deployment.
