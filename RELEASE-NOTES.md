# Archipelago 0.18.0 — Clear paths & familiar faces

Early development build, 22 October 2026. Not a final build, release candidate or final playtest.

- Fixed the dock/woodland return controls ignoring taps during conversations. The labelled Island button dismisses conversation/storage menus, clears the local walk and returns to the current island's overworld.
- Added a visible walk-out marker at each close-up entrance. Tap it to walk back out.
- Larger, high-contrast ground markers with 56 CSS-pixel interactive tap areas. NPC dots are directly beneath their feet and follow their routines. Overlapping tap areas choose the nearest marker; tapping a character's body also approaches them. Markers draw behind the people.
- Unified Hannah and all six existing NPCs under one pixel-character design with matching directional walks and animated pixel portraits. Individual hair, outfits, glasses, hats, beard and build differences remain. Existing player customization is preserved.
- Exiting mid-repair keeps completed work and spent materials; acknowledgement can be resumed without duplicate costs. Research payments, first shrine glow and existing inventories remain.
- Existing NPC routines and temporary ferry exploration after the first sleep remain enabled. Postal/shop/trader schedules are unchanged.

Playable islands remain Old Rayne Isle and Pinewatch. Reedhaven, Minnie, Mona, Euan and wildlife photography remain future chapters. The final-release ferry checkpoint remains in FUTURE-BUILDS.md.

Install: extract this full ZIP into the GitHub repository root, replace matching files, commit and push. No earlier release is needed. Nothing has been deployed automatically.
