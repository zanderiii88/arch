# Production art — 0.27

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The approved Mini/Mona lineup is the character identity/style reference; previous Pinewatch, Reedhaven, workshop and fishing backgrounds are game-style/layout references. No real-person photographs were used for these new witch sprites. Atlas processing only crops transparent gutters, scales with nearest-neighbour sampling and packs frames; it does not redraw/recolour the artwork.

Final files and generation specifications:

- `assets/witches-v27.png`: transparent 1536×1024, four columns/two rows; Mini idle/blink/wave A/wave B on row 1, Mona idle/blink/tea A/tea B on row 2. Equal 384×512 cells, opaque figure height up to 470, foot baseline 500. Match the approved human-witch lineup's warm chunky pixel art, large expressive eyes and simple clothes. Mini: black shoulder-length hair, charcoal tunic/trousers, plum capelet, explorer satchel, dark boots/crooked hat. Mona: older kind face, black bun with silver strands, cream bib/cuffs/socks, charcoal dress, plum shawl, pale shoes/crooked hat. No cat features, text, scenery or ground shadows.
- `assets/emberstone-v27.png`: portrait one-screen volcanic overworld. Match the existing island pixel texture/camera. Basalt coast, ochre paths, sparse hardy shrubs/sea thrift, turquoise sea/foam, cottage upper left, steaming springs upper right, ferry landing lower left, fishing pier lower right; unobstructed connected paths. Distant ruined tower is an inaccessible hint. No characters, UI, icons or text.
- `assets/witch-cottage-v27.png`: portrait cutaway room matching the workshop camera. Two individual beds, tea table left, explorer desk right, central basalt fireplace, cream plaster/herbs/books, warm wooden floor and clear central walking aisle to a bottom doorway. Practical cosy witch home, no characters/text/UI.
- `assets/mineral-shore-v27.png`: portrait overhead flat orange/cream gritty mineral shelf next to a turquoise warm spring, basalt cobbles only at edges, open playable centre, notebook/brush at lower right, pixel style matching Emberstone. No crystals, characters, labels or UI baked into the play area.
- `assets/minerals-v27.png`: transparent 1536×1024 packed 4×2. Top row four differently shaped dusty basalt cobbles with small pale glints; bottom row quartz cluster, orange/cream banded agate, black obsidian with blue/purple glints, honey-cream calcite. Crisp readable pixel silhouettes, natural facets, no magic/text/background.
- `assets/ember-fish-v27.png`: transparent 1536×512 packed 2×1, red gurnard facing right and saithe facing right. Chunky outlined item pixels; gurnard salmon/red with blue-edged pectoral fin, saithe charcoal/olive/silver with pale lateral line and fork tail. All fins/tails visible, no labels/water/shadows.
- `assets/ember-fishing-v27.png`: portrait fishing backdrop with the existing fishing pier's functional layout. Clear wooden dock enters top centre, ends at 26% height; basalt/sea thrift at upper corners, open turquoise water below. No people, bucket, rod, fish, UI or text; all gameplay objects are drawn separately.

The full source remains in the generated-image outputs; these are the project-bound assets consumed by code and cached offline. `ember-v27.js` controls the new renderer, navigation and mineral model; game scene integration is in the versioned main script.
