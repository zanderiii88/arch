# Archipelago 0.123.0 - A place for every sprig

After learning a cooking tip from the cottage, tap Herbs beside the recipe to show the sprigs you are carrying. The herb cards show their food or water perks. Tap Herbs again to return to the regular ingredients where you left them. The cottage notes point to the new button.

The kitchen keeps the pixel-art pan and shows up to six ingredients beside it in two rows of tappable tiles. Tap a tile to take that ingredient out. The kitchen body can scroll on especially short screens so the Cook button can still be reached. Cook something else keeps the last shelf open, and a fresh visit starts on the regular shelf.

# Archipelago 0.122.0 - A sprig for the pot

Through Kyra’s field notes and the existing cottage herb deliveries, Mini and Mona share ways to use one sprig in a pot of soup, stew or borscht. The first basket delivery opens the wild mint tip. Delivering marsh leaf for the cottage opens Mini’s second tip. Look for both in Notes from the cottage. Learned sprigs you already carry appear on the kitchen ingredient shelf. Wild mint adds 12 water when you eat the meal. Marsh leaf adds 12 food.

One sprig is used when cooking succeeds. Seasoned portions have their own backpack stacks and reuse the base dish art with a sprig badge. They can be stored and eaten through the normal inventory. Discovering a seasoned dish teaches its underlying recipe in the cookbook. Standard recipes, cottage request credits and the protected herb-pouch capacity still apply.

# Archipelago 0.121.0 - From plot to pot

After Jamie and Ben’s community timber project, the shack’s More menu opens all three vegetable plots. Plant and water beetroot once, then harvest two for the pantry after three game days. Seed is retained so it can be planted again. Ready crops stay in the plot until you pick them.

Katie sells cabbage for 3 coins and Bruce sells smoked paprika for 9 coins on their existing visits after the timber project. Purchases go to your backpack. Move them to the pantry at home to cook with them. The harvest and vendor descriptions give clues for Try ingredients. Borscht is discovered by successfully cooking beetroot, cabbage, potato and water together. Add smoked paprika to discover Smoky borscht. Discoveries enter the cookbook for repeat cooking, and prepared meals go to the backpack.

Use the cumulative update ZIP over installed 0.114. Extract into the game root, replacing matching files and keeping the other game files. The source checkpoint's dist uses the historical 0.83 recovery baseline.

Native tests cover both save slots, upgrades from saves generated by 0.114 and 0.120, timed harvests, native shop and trader purchases, storage transfers, ingredient and backpack limits, discovery, reload, meal consumption and DEV travel. The new artwork was rasterised and compared with the existing kitchen tiles at 48 and 35 pixels. The current environment blocked Chromium, so browser layout and a live service-worker update still need checking. On a phone, check the three-plot layout, vendor scrolling and buying, tap or drag to the pantry, the ingredient strip, and update/app resume in both slots.
