# Emberstone — first playable section

This early-development release adds a fourth playable island. It is not a release candidate, final build or final playtest.

Travel: speak to Nat at any landing and select Emberstone. The existing temporary exploration setting keeps the ferry and all implemented destinations available after the first sleep; it does not consume or complete repair donations. With that setting disabled, the Emberstone route requires the Reedhaven route and one landing repair kit. Before final release, passenger schedules, all genuine progression gates and emergency-phone/camping rules still need their planned review and implementation.

The island has a distinct basalt/ochre coast, connected walking paths, warm-spring scenery, a mineral shelf and a shared cottage. The little distant tower is scenery and remains inaccessible; Stuart has not been introduced. There is no bathing activity or player accommodation/repair project on Emberstone yet.

Mini explores between the cottage and springs and waves; Mona stays near the doorstep or her tea table and stirs her cup. They move between the overworld and interior and settle inside after 21:00. Their human witch designs echo the cats' colouring. Mini is Mona's granddaughter. Routines are cosmetic and reset safely on reload/return; meetings persist. The illustrated front/three-quarter art has blink, wave and tea poses, gentle breath/bob and mirrored facing; it is not a complete drawn walk-cycle or rear-view animation atlas. The same pixels provide the conversation portraits. Ground markers follow their feet and draw before the characters; header exits work at any time.

Mineral hunting: drag over the six irregular stones to brush away grit. Tap a revealed mineral's round plus marker to record it. Quartz, agate, obsidian and calcite go into persistent field notes, not inventory; the minerals remain in their habitat. There is no time-pressure or failed attempt. Each game day has one fixed six-stone patch; brushing/recording survives closing the activity and reloading. A fresh patch appears only on a new game day. Mona thanks the player with 50 coins once all four minerals have been recorded. The reward cannot repeat after closing/reloading. Open notes through the activity header, Mona or the journal.

The new coastal fishing view offers red gurnard and saithe. Existing casting, no-bite chances, gentle tension/reeling, observation, release, catches, cold storage and research remain shared. The two fish add an Emberstone coastal research milestone. Existing islands, inventory, story and saves remain.

Save behaviour: a reload from the new close-up scenes returns to the island with field-note/patch progress retained. Conversations stop the world clock and boat movement. The new scenes use normal needs/time; forced sleep on Emberstone follows the existing away-island rescue back to the home shack. Reduced-motion mode uses stable character art and removes decorative movement while preserving functional walking/input.

Validation: production-controller tests cover first-sleep travel, all 25 ordered destination routes, lane/collision constraints, NPC approaches and door transitions, conversations/time/boats, interior navigation and exits, pointer brushing/cancellation, duplicate record/reward prevention, partial/full patch saves and day renewal, old-save migration, local fish catch/observation/research, night routines and forced sleep. Regression checks cover crab-pot bait/outcome/landing/claim, earlier household contents, Pinewatch workshop and Reedhaven/Euan. Production canvas renders and atlas frames inspected. No live browser/device test was available; mobile layout/touch feel, sound output and long-session memory need user playtesting.

The accompanying GIF is a silent six-second production-canvas animation preview. Residents are staged together in the cottage for the preview; it is not a recording of their full daily routine or browser interface.
