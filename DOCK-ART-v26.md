# Harbour art and model — 0.26

Project assets: assets/harbour-v26.png, assets/boats-v26.png, assets/crew-v26.png. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. The rocky coast from 0.25 supplies the environment palette; the approved Jamie/Ben preview supplies the character style. An earlier smoother merchant draft was discarded. The selected merchant sheet uses visible pixel clusters and is assembled into 384 × 512 cells, four columns by three rows. Nearest-neighbour resizing and shared waist baselines preserve pixel edges. The harbour renderer projects three vertical strips to align the painted walkway with its collision band. Boat front-rail crops occlude each upper-body sprite without separately generating a mismatched barrier.

Final prompt set:

Harbour: Game environment asset for Archipelago. Wide 2048x1536 pixel-art side-on harbour panorama for a mobile side-scrolling dock. Match the reference's crisp warm cozy pixel clusters and brilliant turquoise ocean. Long empty wooden pier runs horizontally continuously from left to right, flat deck between y960 and y1210, front edge and pilings below to y1340. Shallow three-quarter side view with open continuous walkway, open sea behind, distant island silhouettes and clouds, small sandy/grass bank and path only at far right. No boats, people, text, UI, counters, signs or fences crossing the walkway.

Boats: Transparent pixel-art boat sheet, three 1024x512 cells stacked vertically. All boats face left in the same side-on/slightly top perspective. Cream/teal passenger ferry with red lower hull, cabin at right and empty open foredeck at left; teal shop boat with coral/cream striped awning and clear counter/foredeck; russet wooden trader's launch with green awning, parcels/chest tucked at right and empty foredeck at left. Front bulwarks/rails in each image, no characters, water, wakes, dock, backdrop or lettering. Same scale and generous margins.

Selected crew: GAME SPRITE atlas matching the approved Jamie and Ben simplified pixel rendering: visible square pixel clusters, small flat palette, thick dark pixel outlines and simple friendly adult faces. No smooth cartoon shading, painting, anti-aliased curves or brush texture. Imagine each torso on a 96x128 logical grid enlarged 4x nearest-neighbour. Four columns and three rows on transparent background; all waist-up, aligned within rows. Row 1 Nat: young Asian woman, mid-length black hair, navy captain cap with gold anchor, navy/teal jacket and cream shirt. Row 2 Katie: long blonde hair, cream blouse, coral cardigan, pearl earrings. Row 3 Bruce: wavy brown hair, neat brown beard, olive waistcoat, cream rolled-sleeve shirt and tiny gold horse pin. Columns: neutral hands low, blink, friendly one-hand wave, open-mouth talk with hand gesture. Mostly front with a slight turn to screen right. Same proportions/palette between frames, no props, text, counter or backdrop, true transparent alpha.

Animations are limited torso sets, not full walking or rear-view models. Faces use the same selected sprite art.
