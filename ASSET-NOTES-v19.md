# Reedhaven art notes

New assets were generated with the built-in image-generation tool and copied into the game without PNG reencoding.

- `assets/reedhaven-v19.png`: cozy pixel wetland island with willow trees, reeds, connected boardwalks, a left landing, stilt cottage, field hut, hide and fishing pier; no people, labels or icons. 1024 × 1536.
- `assets/wetland-view-v19.png`: quiet portrait marsh view, open water, reed edges, distant willows, foreground bank and log; no animals, people or interface. 1024 × 1536.
- `assets/wetland-wildlife-v19.png`: transparent sheet of grey heron, Eurasian otter, common kingfisher and emperor dragonfly, each with two behaviour poses. 1536 × 1024.

Euan uses the same code-native pixel-character renderer as Hannah and the existing cast. Photographs are composed from the wetland background and the animal’s saved position/pose, keeping saves small rather than storing image blobs.
