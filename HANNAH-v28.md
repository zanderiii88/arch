# Archipelago 0.28.0 — Hannah comes home

The approved chunky player is now in the creator and the first beach shack. Includes the four-pose walk, idle breathing/blinks, talking and face expressions, a closer wave, and three approved outfits. Character name remains editable and dialogue remains non-gendered. Creator options in this art trial are the approved bob, brown top and three lower garments; other hair/accessory/body combinations await matching artwork.

Try a new game or continue an existing save, go Home, walk around, then use Wardrobe to change clothes or give a little wave. Existing first-entry dialogue uses the animated matching face. Creator Expression and Movement buttons preview all states. The player can still sleep, use the fridge and storage, and leave the shack. The home rendering uses the new artwork even if an existing save uses the previous player art elsewhere.

NPC designs are unchanged. The overworld and other activities keep their existing renderer while the dedicated small sprite and activity poses are developed. No other characters are redesigned in this build. Save key and inventory/progression data are unchanged. Temporary exploration ferry mode remains enabled after the first sleep; restore schedule, gates and emergency-phone rules before final release.

Upload the extracted complete contents to the repository root, replacing matching files. This ZIP includes previous releases' game content; older ZIPs are not required. Do not upload the ZIP itself as the game. Reload and check for v0.28.0 on the menu.
